FAQ

Questions girls & parents ask us

What room types are available?

2 sharing, 3 sharing and 4 sharing rooms. See photos →

What is the monthly rent?

It depends on the sharing type. Call 97315 51839 / 82968 79001 or send an enquiry and we'll share current prices and availability.

Is food included?

Yes. Tiffin (7:30–10:00 AM), lunch (12:30–2:00 PM) and dinner (7:30–10:00 PM), served daily in the dining hall.

How safe is the PG?

It's girls-only, with a biometric lock and CCTV at the main door. Outsiders aren't allowed without permission and the gate closes at 11 PM.

Can my parents or a guest stay over?

Guest accommodation is available at ₹500 per day, with or without food, with prior permission.

What do I need to know before moving out?

Give 30 days' notice, otherwise one month's rent is payable. A one-time ₹2,000 maintenance charge applies when vacating.
